Grievance Policy

A clear and fair process for raising and resolving workplace concerns. Use the informal process first, or submit a formal grievance where the concern can't be resolved informally or needs formal review.

Informal process

  • Discuss the concern with your Team Lead and try to resolve it informally.
  • The Team Lead assesses its seriousness and decides the next step.
  • People Ops becomes involved if requested.

Formal process

  1. Submit the Grievance Form.
  2. Initial review by the Team Lead. If it concerns the Team Lead, it goes to the Department Lead. If it concerns the Department Lead, it goes to People Ops.
  3. The Team Lead seeks to resolve it within one week, otherwise it moves to the Department Lead.
  4. The Department Lead seeks to resolve it within two weeks, otherwise it moves to People Ops.
  5. People Ops seeks to resolve it within three weeks.
  6. Closure: you, the Team Lead and People Ops are informed of the decision.

Appeal

  • You may appeal after People Ops' final decision, in writing to the Executive Board, within five business days.
  • The Executive Board decides within one week.

Confidentiality

Discussions and decisions are documented and shared only with those who need them to review, resolve or administer the grievance.
